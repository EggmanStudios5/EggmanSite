---
title: Mateo Station Mounting Blueprint
description: Reference guidance for battery activation, placement height, and solar panel orientation.
---

1. **Enable Internal Battery Switch:** Unscrew the weatherproof bottom cap on the louvered radiation shield and flip the LiFePO4 battery switch to **ON**.
2. **Select Deployment Topology:** Pair Wi-Fi nodes during initial setup or power on the indoor LoRa receiver hub for long-range deployments.
3. **Mount at 1.5 m – 2.0 m Height:** Place the radiation shield over natural grass or open air—never directly above dark asphalt or HVAC exhaust vents.
4. **Aim Solar Panel South (~45° Tilt):** Maximizes winter irradiance and sheds rain and snow. Allow 24 hours outdoors for MQ-135 baseline stabilization.
